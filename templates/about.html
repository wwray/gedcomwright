About this site

This is a public record of the {{ site.title }} lines, maintained in Family Tree Maker and published here as a plain, self-contained site - no tracking, no third-party services, nothing but the records themselves.

Documents, photographs and sources are shown alongside each person where they exist, grouped by where they came from, so you can see exactly what a fact is based on rather than taking it on faith.

About the people shown here

Only people who have died are published, and only once the tree records that - a death, burial or similar fact with a date, or old enough ancestry/descent to make it certain. Anyone who may still be living is left out entirely: no page, no facts, no photos. Where a living person would otherwise appear in a family listing - as someone's parent, spouse or child - they're shown only as "Living [Surname]", with no link and nothing else about them.
